Privacy notice

Effective November 7, 2026

This notice explains how Clear Pro Pools LLC uses information from clearpropools.com, service inquiries and calls made through the website. Contact us at clearpropoolsllc@gmail.com about this notice or your information.

Service requests

When you prepare a service request, the website creates an email draft on your device containing the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, optional email, ZIP code, requested service and message. The request reaches us only when you send it using your email app. Your email provider also processes that message. We use the details you send to respond to your inquiry and arrange requested services. Please do not include payment details or other sensitive information.

Preparing an email or opening your email app does not confirm that you sent a request. Form field contents are not sent to Google Analytics or captured by CallRail on this website.

Optional website and advertising measurement

If you select Allow measurement, Google Analytics and CallRail use cookies, browser storage and similar technologies to help us understand website use and which marketing efforts lead to inquiries. This can include pages visited, referral sources, campaign and ad-click identifiers, browser and device information, approximate location, timestamps, online identifiers and interactions such as clicking a phone number or preparing an email request.

CallRail can display a different phone number to connect a call with the website visit and marketing source that led to it. Calls still forward to Clear Pro Pools. CallRail processes call information such as the caller's number, call time, duration and connection status. Call recording is off for this website's tracking numbers. Call and attribution events may be shared with Google Analytics and Google Ads to measure results; the website does not send your name, email address, message or personal phone number to Google Analytics.

Measurement is optional. Google Analytics and CallRail's website scripts stay off until you allow them. Declining leaves our regular phone number available and does not prevent service requests. Personalized advertising consent remains off.

Your choices

Use at any time to allow or decline measurement. Your choice is remembered in this browser for up to 180 days. A new browser, cleared storage or an expired choice may prompt you again. We honor the browser's Global Privacy Control signal by disabling optional website measurement.

When you withdraw permission, this website removes its accessible Google and CallRail measurement identifiers and reloads to stop further website tracking. This does not erase records already collected or stop a call already in progress. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information associated with your inquiry, or to ask about retention and other applicable privacy rights. We may need information to verify and locate your request. You can also manage cookies through your browser.

Service providers

Clear Pro Pools and Riva Strategies, our website and marketing service provider, use this information for lead capture, attribution and measurement. Website hosting and communication providers process information needed to deliver their services, including ordinary server logs. Google and CallRail process measurement information when enabled. External sites and your email or messaging provider have their own privacy practices.

Read more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, Google Analytics browser opt-out, and CallRail's privacy practices.

Updates and contact

We will update this notice when our website's practices change. For questions or privacy requests, email clearpropoolsllc@gmail.com or call (281) 824-5239.
